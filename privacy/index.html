Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This website (gearcertified.com and gearcertified.ca) is operated by GearCertified (“we”, “us”, “our”) from Canada. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, how we use it, and the choices you have. Privacy contact: support@gearcertified.com

Information we collect

  • Your email address, when you sign up for our discount code or updates.
  • A record of your consent: the date and time, the wording you agreed to, the version of this policy, and where you signed up.
  • Your name, email address, message, and any attachments, if you email us.
  • Technical data such as your IP address, browser and device type, and the pages you request. Our hosting provider logs this automatically, and Google receives your IP address when your browser loads the fonts used on this site.
  • Small settings saved in your own browser, such as that you signed up, your consent record, and your light or dark mode choice.

We do not collect payment card details or run a checkout on this website. Purchases are made on Amazon.

How and why we use it

  • To give you your discount code after you sign up (to provide what you asked for).
  • To send you product updates, launch news, offers, and other marketing emails (with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time).
  • To answer your messages and give support.
  • To keep proof of consent and unsubscribe requests, and to comply with the law (legal obligation and our legitimate interest in protecting our business).
  • To keep the website secure and working, and to prevent misuse of discount codes (legitimate interests).

Consent

We ask for your consent before sending marketing emails, and you give it by ticking the box on our sign-up form. You can withdraw your consent at any time by using the unsubscribe link in any of our emails or by emailing us. Withdrawing consent does not affect what we did before you withdrew it.

Sharing and service providers

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We share information only with service providers that help us run this website and send email, under contract and only for our purposes, and when required by law or to protect our rights. These currently include:

  • Google, which stores our sign-up list (using Google Sheets) and provides the fonts used on this site.
  • Our website hosting provider, which delivers this site and keeps technical logs.
  • The email service we use to send our emails, once we start sending them.

If we sell or restructure our business, your information may be transferred to the new owner, who must continue to protect it under this policy.

Amazon

Our products are sold on Amazon, and the Amazon links on this website go to our own product listing. This is not an affiliate website. When you click through, you leave this website. Amazon handles your order, payment, shipping, and returns under its own terms and privacy notice, and we do not see your Amazon account or payment details. As a seller, we may receive limited order information from Amazon, which we use only as Amazon allows and to handle your order or support request.

International transfers

We and our providers may process information in countries other than your own, including Canada and the United States. Where the law requires, we use appropriate safeguards for these transfers.

How long we keep it

We keep your email address until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. We may keep a minimal record of your consent and unsubscribe request afterwards so we can honor your choice and meet legal requirements, such as proof of consent under Canada’s anti-spam law. We keep support emails for as long as needed to help you and meet our legal obligations.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your information, to object to or restrict its use, and to withdraw consent. This includes rights under Canadian privacy law (PIPEDA and Alberta’s PIPA), the EU and UK GDPR, and California privacy law. To use any right, email us. We may need to verify your identity, and we will respond within the time required by law. California residents: we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined, and we will not treat you unfairly for using your rights. If you are unhappy with our response, you can complain to your local privacy authority, such as the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Alberta, or your EU or UK data protection authority.

Marketing emails

Every marketing email identifies GearCertified as the sender, includes our contact details and mailing address, and includes a working unsubscribe link. You can also unsubscribe by emailing us. We stop marketing emails within 10 business days of your request, as required by CASL and the U.S. CAN-SPAM Act.

Cookies and browser storage

We do not use advertising, analytics, or tracking cookies. We use your browser’s local storage only to remember that you signed up, your consent, and your light or dark mode choice. You can clear it in your browser settings at any time. We do not track you across other websites, so Do Not Track signals do not change how this site works.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If a breach affects your information and the law requires us to tell you, we will.

Other websites

Our website links to Amazon, to our Linktree page, and to social platforms, and its share buttons open those platforms. They operate under their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for them.

Changes

We may update this policy at any time. The date above shows the latest version. By signing up for our discount code or updates, or by continuing to use this website, you agree to this policy and to any changes we make to it at any time. If you do not agree with a change, you can unsubscribe or ask us to delete your information at any time.
