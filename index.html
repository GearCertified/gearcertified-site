Frequently Asked Questions

Is international shipping available?

Yes. Our grips launch soon on Amazon.com, and international shipping options are available directly through Amazon. You can see which destinations are available, along with delivery costs and times, at checkout on Amazon.

Will I still need lifting straps or cable cuffs?

No. Think of them as straps and cable cuffs in one upgraded piece. Regular straps have to be threaded, looped and twisted around the bar before every set. With our grips, the palm flap stays upright, so you just reach for the bar and wrap it in one quick motion. The steel ring also attaches straight to cable machines, so you skip buying cable cuffs, save money and free up space in your gym bag.

Will they dig into my wrists?

They’re built to prevent it. The wrist cuffs use 7mm thick neoprene padding that supports your wrists and eliminates digging and pinching during heavy lifts, so you can focus on your set instead of the discomfort.

How durable are the steel rings and the material holding them?

Very. Each ring is constructed from welded stainless steel, so it won’t bend or break under heavy cable loads. It’s sewn onto heavy-duty fabric with double box X-stitching, which helps prevent tearing and keeps the ring locked in place, even on your heaviest sets.

Do they work on machines with rubber handles?

Yes. The textured synthetic rubber is designed to grip both bare steel and rubber-coated handles, helping you keep a secure hold even when your hands are sweaty.

Do the grips keep their shape?

Yes. The material is designed to hold its shape, so the palm flap stays upright and ready to wrap when you reach for the bar, making transitions between exercises quick and easy.

What exactly do I get inside my package when I order?

You will receive one pair (a left and a right) of our lifting grips, a reusable frosted ziplock storage pouch, and an instructional user guide to maximize your performance from day one.
